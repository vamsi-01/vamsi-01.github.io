PROJECT / 001⌕

Ask Red Hat

What it does: Ask Red Hat is a conversational knowledge assistant that helps customers and support teams find relevant answers across Red Hat product and support knowledge, instead of searching through scattered documentation manually.

Why it matters: It makes trusted technical knowledge easier to discover and turns information retrieval into a more direct, guided experience.

PROJECT / 002⌘

Subscription Watch Agent

What it does: Subscription Watch gives users a natural-language way to understand their subscription capacity and the instances consuming it. A user can ask questions about available capacity or deployed instances rather than manually navigating raw subscription data.

Why it matters: It makes subscription information easier to interpret and helps users move from raw data to operational insight.

PROJECT / 003▤

KCS Drafting Assistant

What it does: The KCS Drafting Assistant helps support engineers turn the knowledge gained while resolving a customer issue into a structured knowledge article draft that can be reviewed and refined for reuse.

Why it matters: It reduces the friction of capturing support knowledge, helping useful solutions become searchable resources for future cases.

PROJECT / 004◈

Case Summarization

What it does: Case Summarization condenses lengthy support-case histories into a concise account of the issue, investigation and current status, giving an engineer a faster way to understand the context of a case.

Why it matters: Clear summaries can make case reviews and handoffs easier, while evaluation helps check that important details are preserved and unsupported information is not introduced.

PROJECT / 005⬡

MCS AI Assistant

What it does: The MCS AI Assistant provides a conversational way to explore technical knowledge, including documentation for Red Hat OpenShift Service on AWS (ROSA), and get relevant information without manually searching across long documentation sets.

Why it matters: It brings product and platform knowledge closer to the user through a single assistant experience, with authenticated access and context-aware responses.

PROJECT / 006 · IN PROGRESS⟲

Unified Agent Testing Platform

What it does: The Unified Agent Testing Platform is being designed to validate AI agents through their endpoint and capability descriptions. It generates relevant test scenarios, presents the proposed inputs and expected checks, and lets a human approve execution.

Why it matters: It aims to make agent quality checks more repeatable across teams, while avoiding duplicate test generation and keeping execution under human control.
